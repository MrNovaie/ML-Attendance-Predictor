1. Daily "who's in" page

A read-only page you switch to with a button fixed at the bottom left. It has:

A calendar date picker that defaults to today, or the latest weekday if today is a weekend.
The same weekend message as the rest of the app, such as "Sat 3 Oct is a Saturday. No attendance on weekends."
The people grouped as In, Out, Partial day, and Not recorded.

How it fits in: it needs no backend changes. It can use GET /api/attendance?date= and /api/people, and in the frontend a view state in App.jsx switches between the two pages without a router. If you made GET /api/attendance admin-only, non-admins won't see it, so decide whether everyone should.

One decision for you: on a future date, should it say "nothing recorded yet", or show predictions? I'd start with "nothing recorded yet" and keep it simple.
